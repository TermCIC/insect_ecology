About the data & how to cite

ERA5 reanalysis combines observations and modelling on a grid of approximately 0.25° (~25 km). Local terrain and field microclimate can differ. The returned grid coordinate may differ from your requested site.

ET₀ is FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration for a well-watered grass surface. P/ET₀ is reported for the selected period using dates with valid precipitation and ET₀ together; it is not automatically a long-term climate classification.

Means exclude missing values. Totals cover valid observations only and are marked partial when coverage is incomplete. All daily dates and monthly groups follow the source's local calendar. PNG images contain the selected chart; CSV includes daily values, units, and query metadata.
